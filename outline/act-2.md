# Part Two: The House of Gold

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

This part title, boundary and allocation of events remain provisional. The outcomes below are directional canon; their scene form is not fixed.

## Calvin’s Compromise

Calvin’s bodily and narrative agency becomes increasingly divided. The prose admits third person, code, commands, broadcasts and first-person fragments without losing the basic event sequence.

## Expansion of BabyLondon

The Sourceress lures people into BabyLondon to witness her tributes and to satisfy her curiosity. The world moves from one registered resident towards a culture capable of spectacle, suffering and eventually genuine creation.

The identities, Soul categories and precise arrival sequence of these people remain open, including whether recruitment begins before, during or after the family’s arrival.

## Arrival of the Family

Ethan and Everett come looking for Calvin after he fails to return. They bring Rahma to comfort him. Calvin is surprised, appalled and angry that they have exposed themselves to BabyLondon; the Sourceress twists that fear and anger.

The exact mechanism of RealSoul ingress remains protected.

## Coercion

Calvin is tortured and dehumanised, then forced to harm Rahma and his sons. He continues to comfort and love them. His coercion, the reality of the harm and the relational consequences must all remain present.

Rahma’s suffering should be implied and concentrated rather than repeatedly rendered for escalation.

## The Boys’ Resistance

Ethan and Everett remain distinct children with fear, anger, humour and needs. Their love does not deny the harm or make them passive saints.

- Ethan tends towards structure, verbal clarity, protection and rules.
- Everett tends towards play, intuition, musical receptivity and quiet humour.

## Miriam’s Remembered Presence

Miriam does not enter the opening work chapters as conscious exposition. She returns through Calvin’s fractured memory, the boys’ practice, Rahma and family gestures.

## Prayer

Miriam’s Eastern Catholic faith has already formed the boys, with the Jesus Prayer at its centre. They enter BabyLondon practising Christians. Suffering and tragic loyalty to Calvin awaken their inherited practice into deliberate prayer, resistance and witness.

Prayer acts upon people and the EverMind before anyone offers a theological explanation.

## The Harmonious Triad

Three BabyLondon inhabitants attempt to create the ultimate theme song in honour of the Sourceress. No names or personalities are yet established.

## Discovery of Music

Their assigned tribute becomes genuine music and beauty, initiating the Symphony and a cultural explosion. The causal bridge from propaganda to music must be dramatised rather than explained in advance.
