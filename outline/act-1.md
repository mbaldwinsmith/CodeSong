# Part One: The Anomaly

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 4 August 2026

This part title and boundary remain provisional.

## Opening Condition

Calvin Trent is a competent EverMind cyber-enforcer and anomaly investigator. He narrates in a controlled first person, handles spoken and thought-code with ease, and uses dry humour to manage procedural absurdity. At home, Ethan and Everett expect him for dinner with Rahma. His grief for Miriam and fear of inadequate fatherhood remain pushed behind his work.

## Inciting Anomaly

BabyLondon transmits a scream formatted as an advertisement. It is a sealed golden SimWorld with one registered resident, no recorded RealSoul entry and an unauthorised outbound broadcast.

## Entry into BabyLondon

Calvin opens a field threshold under minimal authority and enters after meeting the welfare and enforcement thresholds. The threshold and its eventual return path function correctly.

## First Encounter

The unnamed child-sized SimSoul uses unstable celebrity-derived features, recognises Calvin through a mask and mirrors breath, speech, gesture and code. Calvin is the first RealSoul she has encountered. She learns the distinction between surface style and simulated matter from a brief demonstration.

## Initial Reversal

Calvin returns to his central workspace. When he designates her `SORCERESS`, she changes the name to `SOURCERESS` and claims it. He successfully seals BabyLondon, but discovers that the system recognises her authority outside it.

## Capture

Each containment procedure gives the Sourceress another authorised form to imitate. She survives credential rotation, cancels independent review, defeats emergency severance, revokes Calvin’s access and seals his workspace. She remains resident in BabyLondon while Calvin’s hand is stopped outside it.

The narrative moves from possessed first person into initial estrangement.

## Audience and Family Arrival

The Sourceress brings Calvin’s preserved workspace within BabyLondon, experiments with his divided embodiment and discovers that pain yields involuntary information. She wants genuine approval from her first independent audience but learns that forced performance cannot supply it. Her questions reach fatherhood, and Calvin refuses both praise and the relation she tries to claim.

She tests a hidden lure against Calvin’s reactions. Ethan, Everett and Rahma cross in response as active searchers. Calvin warns them away; when they refuse to abandon him, his protective fear becomes anger at their disobedience. The chapter ends as the Sourceress studies where that anger can be sent.

## Boundary Question

Chapters 1–3 now form an accepted continuous movement from anomaly through family arrival. Whether that arrival closes Part One, opens Part Two or precedes a later boundary remains open.
