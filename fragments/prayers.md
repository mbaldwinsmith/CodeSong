# Prayers

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 4 August 2026

## Purpose

Collect unattached prayer fragments while preserving their speaker, source and narrative context.

## Established Context

- Miriam was Eastern Catholic.
- The Jesus Prayer was central to her practice with Ethan and Everett.
- The boys prayed it with her before her death.
- They continue as practising Christians and attend church with Calvin, who does not believe but honours Miriam by taking them.
- Their suffering and tragic love for Calvin awaken their inherited faith into deliberate prayer and witness in BabyLondon.
- Prayer has material and relational effects within the EverMind without theological exposition.

## Exact Text

Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me.

This is the canonical root form Miriam taught Ethan and Everett. Do not add “a sinner” to their inherited wording unless a later scene gives an individual boy reason to choose it for himself.

## Calvin’s Later Addition

When Calvin freely accepts the Chorus’s offer, he becomes the first character to pray:

> Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner.

The addition belongs to Calvin’s confession of responsibility and need for mercy. It is not imposed, and the other voices need not repeat it. Their shorter form may continue around his distinct line.
