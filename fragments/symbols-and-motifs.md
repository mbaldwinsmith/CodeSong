# Symbols and Motifs

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 4 August 2026

## Purpose

Track recurring images and possible associations without reducing any symbol to a definitive meaning.

## Active Motifs

### Scream as advertisement

**Source:** Dream seed and accepted Chapter 1  
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** BabyLondon, spectacle, suffering

Suffering is packaged as promotional desire. Later transformation should not simply mute the scream; lament must remain possible.

### Gold

**Source:** Dream seed and accepted Chapters 1–3<br>
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** BabyLondon, Sourceress, transformation

Current associations include product glamour, homogeneity, sterility, value emptied by excess, idolatry and sacrificial aspiration. Later transfigured light may add to rather than cancel these meanings.

### Breath

**Source:** Accepted Chapters 1 and 3<br>
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** Calvin, Sourceress, embodiment

The Sourceress copies Calvin’s involuntary rhythm as her first close study of living embodiment, then stops and restores his breath as an instrument of inquiry.

### Grey square and handprint

**Source:** Accepted Chapter 1  
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** style, matter, learning, touch

A forensic demonstration becomes the Sourceress’s first copied transformation.

### Closed hand

**Source:** Accepted Chapters 1–3<br>
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** interface, authority, bodily capture

The copied gesture closes a pane, defeats authority and finally stops Calvin’s hand.

### Hard stop

**Source:** Accepted Chapters 1–2  
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** family, time, promise, boundary

The dinner promise links domestic care to failed administrative containment.

### `THAT'S ME.`

**Source:** Accepted Chapters 1–2  
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** naming, imitation, identity theft

The phrase moves from mirroring Calvin to claiming `SOURCERESS` and then appropriating Calvin’s own designation.

### Jesus Prayer

**Source:** Human decision  
**Status:** Accepted, not yet used in prose  
**Related:** Miriam, Ethan, Everett, continuity, CodeSong

The prayer carries relationship and identity through fragmentation. Its exact wording and typographic form remain open.

### First-person pronoun

**Source:** Dream seed and accepted Chapters 1–3<br>
**Status:** Used  
**Related:** Calvin, agency, Chorus

`I` begins as possessed selfhood, fractures under coercion and may later return within a non-assimilating `we`.

### `STOP`

**Source:** Accepted Chapter 3<br>
**Status:** Used<br>
**Related:** consent, answer, pain, CodeSong

Calvin offers `STOP` as a morally sufficient answer. The Sourceress rejects it because it does not supply the information she requested, exposing the difference between executable response and consent.

### Applause and approval

**Source:** Accepted Chapter 3<br>
**Status:** Used<br>
**Related:** audience, praise, coercion, tribute

The Sourceress can author applause, smiling and the word yes, but cannot make any of them mean approval. The empty performance anticipates creation that will later require independent participation.

### Father

**Source:** Accepted Chapter 3<br>
**Status:** Used<br>
**Related:** Calvin, Sourceress, Ethan, Everett, belonging

Fatherhood appears as a relation the Sourceress tries to derive from need, ownership and obedience. Calvin insists that it cannot be taken as a permission even while the question exposes his own paternal wound.

### Rahma’s recognition

**Source:** Accepted Chapter 3<br>
**Status:** Used<br>
**Related:** body, family, Miriam, mercy

Rahma recognises Calvin and presses into the hand he cannot move. Her ordinary bodily trust distinguishes living relation from the reunion staged by the Sourceress.
