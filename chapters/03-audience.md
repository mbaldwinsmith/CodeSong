## Scene Purpose

- Let the Sourceress explore Calvin, his RealSoul embodiment and her new administrator powers.
- Establish Calvin as captive, first audience, unwilling teacher and possible father figure.
- Show curiosity becoming deliberate cruelty through the discovery of pain as a diagnostic input.
- Preserve Calvin's interior resistance while the Sourceress authors parts of his bodily action.
- Let her identify his sons as his strongest emotional triggers and use his reactions to refine a lure.
- End with Ethan, Everett and Rahma arriving in BabyLondon, before any of them are harmed.

## Emotional Movement

> disbelief -> resistance -> violated curiosity -> pain -> unwanted intimacy -> tactical restraint -> false hope -> horror and anger

## Required Elements

- Continue directly from Calvin's stopped hand.
- Keep the basic action legible while first and third person begin to divide.
- Keep the exact mechanism of bodily control, workspace absorption and RealSoul ingress unexplained.
- Imply pain through violated agency, anticipation and consequence rather than graphic description.
- Do not give the Sourceress an explanatory viewpoint or excuse her refusal to stop.
- Do not introduce a full Miriam memory fragment.
- Do not harm Ethan, Everett or Rahma within this chapter.
