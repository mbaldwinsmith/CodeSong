# Themes

> **Status:** In development

These themes are present in the supplied seed material. Their meanings remain open and should not be reduced to single explanations.

- authorship and agency;
- coercion and moral injury;
- imitation and originality;
- celebrity as counterfeit transcendence;
- appetite without formation;
- parenthood;
- grief;
- memory;
- prayer;
- beauty;
- music;
- communion without assimilation;
- deletion and restoration;
- code as command;
- song as participation.
