## Established Contrasts

| Administrator paradigm | CodeSong paradigm |
|---|---|
| authenticated command | consensual participation |
| authority acts upon an object | voices prompt reality together |
| direct recoding | calling forth possibilities |
| can be stolen or misapplied | cannot occur without participation |
| technical power | relational creation |

Administrator privileges may later support the Chorus at scale, but they are not identical to CodeSong and do not supply its moral character.
