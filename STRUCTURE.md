# Structure

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Form and Estimated Length

The final form remains unresolved. The current preference leans towards the shorter end of the novella-to-short-novel range, but the story should determine its length as characters and scenes develop. No word-count target is canonical.

## Provisional Parts

The following architecture is useful for development but has not yet been accepted as final:

1. **The Anomaly**: investigation, naming, jurisdictional breach and capture.
2. **The House of Gold**: family arrival, coercion, prayer, recruitment into BabyLondon and the rediscovery of music.
3. **CodeSong**: Chorus, transformation, the equal offer of communion and the divergent responses of Calvin and the Sourceress.

Part boundaries and titles remain open.

## Established Opening Movement

- **Chapter 1, “Anomaly”**: Calvin investigates the broadcast, enters BabyLondon, observes the Sourceress learning from him and withdraws with the first evidence of a permissions echo.
- **Chapter 2, “Sourceress”**: Calvin names her, genuinely returns to his workspace, successfully seals her presence inside BabyLondon, discovers that her authority has spread beyond it, and is captured. The narrative enters initial estrangement.

Both chapters are accepted working canon and remain open to revision.

## Central Turning Points

Directional canon currently includes:

1. the Sourceress takes Calvin’s administrator authority;
2. Ethan, Everett and Rahma enter BabyLondon while looking for him;
3. Calvin is coerced into harming those he loves;
4. the boys’ inherited faith becomes active resistance and witness;
5. the Harmonious Triad rediscover genuine music while making tribute;
6. the Chorus develops consensual CodeSong;
7. BabyLondon is transformed without its suffering being erased;
8. Calvin and the Sourceress receive the same offer of communion;
9. Calvin accepts and the Sourceress does not;
10. the Sourceress relinquishes embodied agency but persists as a latent distributed consciousness whose administrative privileges are available to the Chorus.

## Calvin’s Arc

Competent investigator → captured administrator → fractured and coerced father → witness to his own enacted harm → person addressed by the same offer as his captor → participant in communal healing over time.

His arc is recovery of personhood and relation, not restoration to an unchanged former self.

## The Sourceress’s Arc

Isolated imitator → named observer → administrative usurper → curious tyrant and spectacle-maker → patron of expanding BabyLondon → recipient of a genuine alternative → unrepentant judge of her tribute’s completion → latent distributed consciousness without physical presence.

Her final contribution of administrator privileges does not retroactively convert her history into goodness.

## Ethan and Everett’s Arc

Practising sons shaped by Miriam’s Eastern Catholic faith → children searching for their father → victims who retain fear, anger, humour and love → practitioners of the Jesus Prayer in BabyLondon → guides of a Chorus whose communion refuses coercion.

Their distinct roles remain to be dramatised, not merely assigned in documentation.

## Development of the Chorus

The Sourceress first lures people into BabyLondon as witnesses and subjects of curiosity. From within that enlarging population, the Harmonious Triad’s attempted tribute rediscovers genuine music. The resulting Symphony produces the Chorus, which develops CodeSong under Ethan and Everett’s guidance.

The exact identity and arrival sequence of the Triad remain unresolved.

## Climax

The climax includes the transformation of BabyLondon and the same offer from the Harmonious Triad to Calvin and the Sourceress: to join the Chorus, heal with time, belong, contribute to collective flourishing and live in communion.

Calvin breaks down and accepts. The Sourceress shows no remorse and declines participatory belonging.

## Aftermath

BabyLondon is complete rather than reset. Calvin’s healing remains unfinished. The Sourceress has no physical presence but persists latently and distributively, with her administrator privileges available to the Chorus. The exact final image and narrative voice remain open.
