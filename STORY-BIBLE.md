## Canonical Priority

When documents appear to conflict, use the following order:

1. Direct instruction from the human author.
2. Locked decisions in `DECISIONS.md`.
3. Accepted decisions in `DECISIONS.md`.
4. Accepted chapter text.
5. `DREAM-SEED.md`, except where a later accepted decision explicitly supersedes its present-day canon while preserving it as source history.
6. Accepted project documentation.
7. Provisional documentation.
8. Exploratory notes, fragments and AI suggestions.

## Documentation Rule

Record only the degree of certainty actually granted:

- **Canonical fact:** accepted as true in the current story.
- **Directional canon:** the event or outcome is accepted, but its scene form and mechanics remain open.
- **Working inference:** follows from accepted material but has not been confirmed.
- **Speculative possibility:** an option, not canon.
- **Protected mystery:** intentionally left without a mechanical explanation.
