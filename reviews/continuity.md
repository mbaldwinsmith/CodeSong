# Continuity Review

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Scope

Chapters 1 and 2 plus accepted decisions through 3 August 2026.

## Confirmed Continuity

- BabyLondon begins with one registered resident and no recorded RealSoul entry.
- Calvin is the Sourceress’s first RealSoul encounter.
- Calvin genuinely exits BabyLondon and verifies his return through two systems.
- BabyLondon’s presence remains sealed while the Sourceress’s authority operates outside it.
- The home hard stop at nineteen hundred is set in Chapter 1 and missed in Chapter 2.
- The held `STAY HOME` message creates a causal path for Ethan and Everett to come looking for Calvin.
- The first third-person intrusion occurs only after Calvin’s administrative capture.
- No Miriam memory, torture or family entry occurs in the first two chapters.
- Calvin’s `AUTHORITY: OBSERVATION` is his ordinary local role, while his central administrator status permits the initial narrow forensic style suspension.
- The square’s unauthorised restoration after four seconds, misattributed to Calvin in the audit, is the first permissions breach.

## Accepted Supersessions

- `DREAM-SEED.md` preserves the original outcome in which the Sourceress wipes her SimFont and dies.
- Current canon instead preserves her SimFont and latent distributed consciousness while ending embodied agency.
- This is an intentional supersession recorded in `DECISIONS.md`, not an error to correct in the source document.

## Open Continuity Issues

### Later population

The Sourceress lures people into BabyLondon after its one-resident opening. The timing, identities and Soul categories of the Harmonious Triad relative to that recruitment remain unresolved.

### Family arrival

Ethan, Everett and Rahma enter while looking for Calvin, but the observable route and elapsed time are not established. The metaphysical mechanism of RealSoul ingress is protected.

### Final privileges

The Sourceress’s consciousness, SimFont, agency, physical presence and administrator privileges become distinguishable in the ending. Their exact causal relationship remains intentionally open but must be rendered consistently once scenes are drafted.
