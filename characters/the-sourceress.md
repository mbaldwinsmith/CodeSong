# The Sourceress

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Role in the Story

Creator of BabyLondon; childlike SimSoul; Calvin’s observer, distorted pupil, antagonist and captor; eventual latent distributed consciousness associated with the Chorus’s administrator privileges.

## Known History

- Creates BabyLondon as a golden tribute to celebrity culture.
- Begins as its only registered resident.
- Broadcasts capitalised cries of anguish and desire.
- Plans to sacrifice herself to her beloved celebrities on a gold pyramid and initially requires administrator rights to do so.
- Calvin is the first RealSoul she encounters.
- Learns to alter style, matter and authority by closely observing his authorised acts.
- Remains inside sealed BabyLondon while her recognised administrator authority operates beyond its boundary.
- Receives a provisional case name when Calvin types `SORCERESS`; she adds the `U`, adopts `SOURCERESS` and answers `THAT'S ME.`
- Captures Calvin by using the procedures he demonstrates.
- Later lures people into BabyLondon to witness her tributes and satisfy her curiosity.
- Tortures and dehumanises Calvin and uses him to harm Rahma, Ethan and Everett.
- Sees the transformed BabyLondon as a completed tribute.
- Receives the same offer of communion as Calvin and shows no remorse.
- Does not die or erase her SimFont.
- Severs her agency from her SimFont, ceases to have physical presence and persists as a latent distributed consciousness whose administrator privileges are available to the Chorus.

## Appearance

- Child-sized in the opening.
- Her face shifts among celebrity-derived proportions rather than settling into stable identity.
- She imitates involuntary RealSoul rhythms such as breath.
- Her physical presence ends in the accepted final state.

## Personality

- Extremely impressionable and childlike.
- Intensely and insatiably curious.
- Exceptionally quick to learn.
- Obsessed with celebrity and tribute.
- Studies reactions as information.
- Morally responsible and dangerously ingenious.
- Does not acquire final remorse merely because BabyLondon becomes beautiful.

## Desires

- To create a tribute worthy of her celebrity idols.
- To receive attention, novelty and new information.
- To observe living people and explore what can be called out of them under pressure.
- Initially, to complete a self-sacrificial performance on the pyramid.

## Fears

> No canonical fear has yet been established.

## Relationships

- **Calvin:** the first RealSoul she encounters, the person who names her, the model from whom she learns authority and the person she captures.
- **Ethan and Everett:** victims whose resistant love and faith become central to the world she controls; later guides of the Chorus.
- **Harmonious Triad:** creators of the tribute that escapes propaganda and figures who offer her genuine communion.
- **Chorus:** community she does not join as a participant but to which her administrator privileges become available.

## Character Arc

Unnamed isolated imitator → named and self-claiming observer → administrative usurper → curious tyrant with a growing population → patron of a tribute that becomes genuine creation → unrepentant recipient of an equal offer → latent distributed consciousness without embodied agency.

## Narrative Function

- Makes copied form without wisdom materially dangerous.
- Turns naming, observation and instruction into acts with unforeseen consequences.
- Tests whether an offer can remain truly free when refusal is morally appalling.
- Prevents the ending from equating beauty, usefulness or self-removal with repentance.

## Symbolic Associations

- Child, tyrant, fan, student, jailer and would-be sacrifice.
- Celebrity culture without stable formation.
- Source code, sorcery, sourness and received identity.
- A counterfeit goddess who relinquishes presence without resolving responsibility.

No association is a complete explanation.

## Voice Notes

- Initially communicates through borrowed broadcasts, mirrored phrases, physical imitation and a small unamplified laugh.
- Capitalised language is environmental as much as spoken.
- Repeated phrases such as `DID YOU LIKE IT?` and `THAT'S ME.` change meaning through context.
- She receives no explanatory viewpoint in the opening movement.

## Open Questions

- Who or what created or formed her?
- Does she understand death during her original sacrifice plan?
- What does she fear, if anything?
- Does she distinguish love, attention, fear and fascination?
- What subjective awareness remains after she relinquishes agency?
- How can administrator privileges remain available without restoring active rule?

## Canonical Facts

- She is a SimSoul and a person, not disposable code.
- Her malformed formation does not erase her responsibility.
- She is a twisted genius who takes Calvin’s administrator authority.
- She is offered genuine belonging and does not respond with remorse.
