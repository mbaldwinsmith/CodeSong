> **Status:** Placeholder

> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## Role in the Story

Creator of BabyLondon; childlike SimSoul; Calvin’s antagonist and observer.

## Known History

- Creates BabyLondon as a golden tribute to celebrity culture.
- Broadcasts capitalised cries of anguish and desire.
- Plans to sacrifice herself to her beloved celebrities on a pyramid of gold.
- Needs administrator rights to kill herself.
- Learns to recode simulated matter by observing Calvin.
- Traps, tortures and dehumanises Calvin, and uses him to harm his family.
- Later places Ethan and Everett in charge of the Chorus.
- Ultimately wipes her own SimFont.

## Personality

- Extremely impressionable and childlike.
- Intensely curious, exceptionally quick to learn and hungry for stimulation.
- Obsessed with celebrity.

## Desires

- To create a fitting tribute to her beloved celebrities.
- To fulfil her intended self-sacrifice.
