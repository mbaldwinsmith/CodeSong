# Rahma

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 4 August 2026

## Role in the Story

The Trent family’s yellow Labrador puppy, a living bond with Miriam, a comic presence in the opening home life and later the second tortured soul in BabyLondon.

## Known History

- Is adopted after Miriam learns that her cancer is terminal.
- Is intended to bring life, comfort and companionship after Miriam’s death.
- Steals an item in Chapter 1 and appears with Calvin’s missing sock in Chapter 2.
- Is jokingly named lead complainant when Calvin misses dinner.
- Pulls Everett into view at BabyLondon’s boundary event and is brought by both boys to comfort Calvin.
- Recognises Calvin immediately, reaches his knees and presses her head against his motionless hand while he is unable to touch her.
- Is tortured and killed by Calvin under the Sourceress’s coercion.
- Is repeatedly resuscitated by the Sourceress.
- Is continually soothed and comforted by Calvin while he is forced to harm her.

## Personality

- Playful and acquisitive around household objects.
- Trusted and loved as an ordinary family puppy before she bears symbolic weight.

Do not over-humanise her voice or reduce her to innocence as a plot instrument.

## Desires

> No interior desire beyond ordinary puppy attachment and comfort has been canonically narrated.

## Relationships

- Belongs to Calvin, Ethan and Everett.
- Was brought into the family by Miriam.
- Is brought by the boys to comfort Calvin.

## Character Arc

Family life and comic attachment → attempted comfort in BabyLondon → victim of coerced violence and abusive resuscitation → unresolved survival or memorial future.

## Narrative Function

Rahma binds Miriam’s gift of life to Calvin’s most severe moral injury. Her suffering must not become repetitive spectacle.

## Symbolic Associations

The name “Rahma” evokes mercy, compassion, tenderness and womb-like care. This association is suggestive rather than definitive.

## Open Questions

- What kind of consciousness does Rahma have within the EverMind?
- Does she survive in any form?
- How much of her suffering appears directly on the page?
- Can CodeSong honour or accompany her without counterfeiting resurrection?

## Canonical Facts

- Rahma is a yellow Labrador puppy.
- Repeated revival is not restoration.
- She is a loved family member before she becomes a symbol or victim.

## Speculative Possibilities
