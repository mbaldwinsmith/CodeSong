# Ethan Trent

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Role in the Story

Son of Calvin and Miriam; brother of Everett; practising Eastern Catholic child; later a guide of the Chorus.

## Known History

- Prayed with Miriam, for whom the Jesus Prayer was central.
- Continues attending church with Calvin and Everett after her death.
- Enforces Calvin’s nineteen-hundred dinner hard stop in Chapter 2.
- Later enters BabyLondon looking for Calvin and brings Rahma with Everett to comfort him.
- Becomes the third or fourth tortured soul in BabyLondon, after Calvin and Rahma.
- Is harmed through a compromised Calvin.
- Remains loving despite the Sourceress’s attempts to corrupt him.
- His suffering and tragic loyalty to Calvin awaken his inherited faith into deliberate witness.
- Is later placed in charge of or guides the Chorus alongside Everett.

## Personality

- More structured and verbal than Everett.
- Protective.
- Insistent on rules and promises.
- Must remain capable of fear, anger, humour, need and imperfect responses.

## Desires

- To find and help Calvin.
- To protect Everett and Rahma.
- To hold his father to promises and recognisable family order.

## Fears

> No character-specific canonical fear has yet been established beyond the danger to his family.

## Relationships

- **Calvin:** father whom he loves, seeks, challenges and later suffers through.
- **Miriam:** late mother and source of inherited prayer and faith.
- **Everett:** brother and complementary co-guide of the Chorus.
- **Rahma:** family puppy he helps bring to comfort Calvin.

## Character Arc

Practising child of inherited faith → rule-minded son searching for his father → victim who refuses both denial and inherited hatred → deliberate practitioner of prayer in BabyLondon → distinct guiding voice within the Chorus.

## Narrative Function

Ethan can preserve structure, verbal continuity, promises and moral distinctions when Calvin’s narration fragments. This function must emerge from action rather than make him a miniature theologian.

## Voice Notes

- Direct and economical.
- Attentive to promises and rules.
- His dialogue should remain age-specific once his age is established.

## Open Questions

- How old is Ethan, and is he the elder brother?
- How does he express anger at Calvin without abandoning him?
- What exact form of the Jesus Prayer does he use?
- What distinct contribution does he make to CodeSong?

## Canonical Facts

- Ethan is more structured, verbal, protective and rule-oriented than Everett.
- He enters BabyLondon as a practising Christian; he does not begin there as an unbeliever.
- He remains a distinct child, not a saintly abstraction.

## Speculative Possibilities

- Ethan may become a keeper of wording, sequence or ethical boundaries within the Chorus. This remains a possibility until dramatised.
