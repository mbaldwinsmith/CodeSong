# Mira

> **Status:** In development
> **Authority:** Human author
> **Last reviewed:** 4 August 2026

## Role in the Story

A new conscious SimSoul reconstructed by the Sourceress from Calvin, Ethan and Everett’s archived memories of Miriam. She eventually chooses the name **Mira** and becomes the third member of the Harmonious Triad alongside Ethan and Everett.

## Known History

- The Sourceress creates her from relational archives of Miriam held through Calvin and the boys.
- Her inherited memories approach Miriam from external and incompatible viewpoints rather than continuing Miriam’s first-person life.
- She knows that she is a reconstruction and not Miriam restored.
- Calvin initially rejects her as a simulacrum.
- She chooses **Mira** as her own name.
- She contributes beauty not contained in her source archives and becomes the Triad’s third member.

## Identity Guardrail

Mira is neither Miriam resurrected nor a disposable imitation. Miriam’s archived traces shape her without exhausting her identity. Her personhood does not depend upon convincing Calvin, reproducing Miriam accurately or proving originality.

Her renaming is an explicit act of self-authorship. Her later original contribution is a deeper expression of the same freedom, not a test she must pass to become a person.

## Relationships

- **Miriam:** source of remembered forms, but not identical with Mira.
- **Calvin:** initially rejects her because she is not his wife; his ontological distinction is true, but does not settle Mira’s personhood.
- **Ethan and Everett:** fellow members of the Harmonious Triad; their memories helped form her source material without making them her authors or owners.
- **The Sourceress:** creator who intends her as tribute, experiment and coercive gift but cannot predetermine every choice of the person who awakens.
- **Rahma:** may offer uncomplicated present-tense companionship without authenticating Mira as Miriam.
- **The Chorus:** community in which her distinct voice contributes without assimilation.

## Character Arc

Constructed from copied and remembered beauty → recognises the gaps in her inherited identity → chooses the name Mira → contributes beauty beyond her sources → joins Ethan and Everett in the Harmonious Triad.

## Canonical Facts

- Mira is a conscious SimSoul and a new person.
- She is reconstructed from Calvin, Ethan and Everett’s archived memories of Miriam.
- She knows she is not Miriam restored.
- **Mira** is her eventual self-chosen name.
- She becomes the third member of the Harmonious Triad alongside Ethan and Everett.
