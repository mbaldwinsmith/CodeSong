# The Harmonious Triad

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Role in the Story

Three inhabitants of BabyLondon whose attempt to compose its ultimate tribute song leads to genuine music, the Symphony and the Chorus. They later offer the same communal future to Calvin and the Sourceress.

## Known History

- Exist within BabyLondon after its population begins to grow.
- Compose in honour of the Sourceress.
- Rediscover genuine music and beauty while attempting tribute.
- Their work leads to a cultural explosion and the formation of the Chorus.
- Create or initiate the Symphony.
- Offer Calvin and the Sourceress the same invitation: join the Chorus, heal with time, belong, contribute to collective flourishing and live in communion.
- Calvin accepts their offer; the Sourceress does not accept it as a participant.

## Personality

> No member names, personalities or distinct voices have been established.

## Desires

- Initially, to create the ultimate BabyLondon theme song in honour of the Sourceress.
- Later, to extend genuine belonging and participation without coercion.

## Relationships

- **The Sourceress:** initial patron or object of tribute; later recipient of an offer they do not soften or force.
- **Calvin:** wounded recipient who breaks down and accepts.
- **Ethan and Everett:** guides of the Chorus their discovery helps form.
- **Chorus:** communal consequence of the Symphony.

## Character Arc

Tribute-makers within spectacle → discoverers of music → catalysts of cultural transformation → representatives of a communion capable of making the same offer to victim and perpetrator without equating their histories.

## Narrative Function

- Beauty escapes the propagandistic purpose assigned to it.
- Their offer demonstrates that consent includes the possibility of refusal.
- They bridge music, CodeSong and the communal alternative to administrator rule.
