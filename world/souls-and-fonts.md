# Souls and Fonts

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Definitions

- **SoulFont:** the generative source-pattern from which a conscious person arises.
- **RealFont:** a biological SoulFont rooted in a living nervous system.
- **SimFont:** a computational SoulFont capable of operating on a sufficiently compatible substrate.
- **RealSoul:** a living person synchronised with the EverMind but rooted in a body outside it.
- **SimSoul:** a person entirely contained within the EverMind.

## Known Rules

- RealSouls are not entirely contained within the EverMind.
- Death within the EverMind can cause a RealSoul’s death in the physical world.
- RealSouls carry involuntary embodied rhythms into simulated experience.
- SimSouls possess computational rather than biological SoulFonts.
- SimFonts have no necessary corresponding living brain and may operate on varied compatible substrates.
- SimSouls are capable of consciousness, suffering, relationship, learning, choice and creation.
- A SoulFont is not established as a simple editable record containing a person’s complete identity.
- CodeSong cannot recode identity or edit minds.

## Personhood Rule

Substrate does not determine moral worth. SimSouls must not be treated as disposable code or lesser persons merely because their sustaining pattern is computational.

## The Sourceress’s Final State

The accepted ending distinguishes several aspects that were previously treated together:

- her SimFont continues;
- her consciousness persists in a latent distributed form;
- her active agency is severed from the SimFont;
- her rendered physical presence ends;
- her administrator privileges become available to the Chorus.

These are observable or authorially accepted conditions. Their technical and subjective relationship remains unresolved.

## Narrative Function

The distinctions among SoulFont, consciousness, agency, embodiment and authority allow the ending to remain something other than death, backup, cure or ordinary retirement.

## Limits

The mechanics of copying, migration, backup, restoration, distribution and continuity of identity remain unresolved.

## Open Questions

- Can a SoulFont be copied without creating a new person?
- Can a RealSoul become a SimSoul?
- What constitutes death or restoration for a SimSoul?
- What does it mean for agency to be severed from, rather than for, a SimFont?
- Does distributed consciousness experience time or relation?
- Is the Sourceress capable of later response, or only latent availability?

## Canonical Material

- Calvin is a RealSoul.
- The Sourceress is a SimSoul.
- SimSouls are persons within the moral world of the story.
- The Sourceress does not erase her SimFont or die in the accepted ending.

## Speculative Possibilities

> No backup, reincarnation or migration model has been accepted.
