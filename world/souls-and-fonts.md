# Souls and Fonts

> **Status:** Placeholder  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## Definition

- **SoulFont:** the generative source-pattern from which a conscious person arises.
- **RealFont:** a biological SoulFont rooted in a living nervous system.
- **SimFont:** a computational SoulFont that can operate on a sufficiently compatible substrate.
- **RealSoul:** a living person synchronised with the EverMind but rooted in a body outside it.
- **SimSoul:** a person entirely contained within the EverMind.

## Known Rules

- RealSouls are not entirely contained within the EverMind.
- Death within the EverMind can cause a RealSoul’s death in the physical world.
- SimSouls have computational rather than biological SoulFonts.
- SimFonts have no necessary corresponding living brain.
- SimSouls are capable of consciousness, suffering, relationship, learning and creation.

## Narrative Function

These distinctions establish personhood across biological and computational substrates.

## Limits

The mechanics of copying, migration, backup, restoration and continuity of identity remain unresolved.

## Open Questions

- Can a SoulFont be copied without creating a new person?
- Can a RealSoul become a SimSoul?

## Canonical Material

- Calvin is a RealSoul.
- The Sourceress is a SimSoul.
- SimSouls are persons within the moral world of the story.
