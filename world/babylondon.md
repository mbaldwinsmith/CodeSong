# BabyLondon and SimWorlds

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Definition

A SimWorld is a bounded experiential world operating within the EverMind. BabyLondon is the Sourceress’s golden SimWorld, created as a tribute to celebrity consumer culture.

## Initial State

- One registered resident: the unnamed SimSoul later called the Sourceress.
- No recorded RealSoul entry before Calvin.
- External access closed and outbound broadcast active without permission.
- World purpose registered as `TRIBUTE`.
- A city rendered in uniform product gold beneath a blank gold sky.
- Golden avenues, towers, screens, terraces and a central pyramid.
- Faces derived from celebrity aesthetics but stripped of stable personhood.
- Cries of anguish and desire formatted as advertisements and capitalised slogans.
- Sound suppressed by the environment except where the Sourceress permits or produces it.

## Known Rules and Behaviours

- SimWorlds may contain RealSouls and SimSouls and possess local rules, aesthetics, permissions and populations.
- BabyLondon’s world-owner can alter local style and matter.
- The world boundary can be sealed while its owner’s recognised administrator authority operates elsewhere.
- The field threshold used by Calvin works correctly in both directions.
- BabyLondon later gains inhabitants because the Sourceress lures people to witness her tributes and to satisfy her curiosity.
- Ethan, Everett and Rahma later enter while searching for Calvin; the exact ingress mechanism remains protected.
- The Harmonious Triad and Chorus emerge within the enlarged world.
- CodeSong transforms BabyLondon without erasing its suffering or recoding its inhabitants’ identities.

## Population Movement

- The Sourceress begins alone.
- Calvin becomes the first recorded RealSoul visitor and first captive.
- Ethan, Everett and Rahma later enter in search of Calvin.
- The Sourceress also lures people as witnesses and subjects of curiosity.
- The Harmonious Triad and eventual Chorus arise within the growing population.

The order and overlap of family arrival and wider recruitment, and the Soul categories of later inhabitants, remain unresolved.

## Transformation

The Chorus sings or prompts a new world of immense beauty and harmony. The transformed BabyLondon exceeds the celebrity culture it was built to imitate and becomes complete in the Sourceress’s judgement.

Completion does not mean:

- deletion of history;
- automatic healing of inhabitants;
- proof of the Sourceress’s goodness;
- assimilation into one collective voice.

## Final State

BabyLondon continues after the Sourceress relinquishes embodied agency. She no longer has physical presence but persists as a latent distributed consciousness. The world’s exact boundaries, governance and future relation to the wider EverMind remain open.

## Open Questions

- What makes it “Baby”?
- What attracts later people strongly enough to enter?
- Are the Harmonious Triad among those deliberately lured?
- What does transformed gold look, sound and feel like?
- Does BabyLondon remain a distinct SimWorld after the Chorus’s transformation?
