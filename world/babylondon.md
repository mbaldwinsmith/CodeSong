# BabyLondon and SimWorlds

> **Status:** Placeholder  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## Definition

A SimWorld is a bounded experiential world operating within the EverMind. BabyLondon is the Sourceress’s golden SimWorld, created as a tribute to celebrity consumer culture.

## Known Rules

- SimWorlds may contain RealSouls and SimSouls.
- SimWorlds may possess their own rules, aesthetics, permissions and populations.
- BabyLondon is dominated by gold and contains a gold pyramid intended for the Sourceress’s self-sacrifice.
- BabyLondon broadcasts cries of anguish and desire resembling spam, advertisements or capitalised celebrity language.
- The Chorus later transforms BabyLondon through CodeSong.

## Narrative Function

BabyLondon is the anomalous world Calvin investigates and the principal site of the supplied narrative movement.

## Limits

The permissions, exit conditions and technical boundaries of SimWorlds have not been established.
