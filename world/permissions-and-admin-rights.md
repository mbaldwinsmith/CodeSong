# Permissions and Administrator Rights

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Definition

Administrator rights are authenticated permissions used by Calvin in his EverMind work. They allow actions such as altering simulated matter, changing style layers, sealing worlds, suspending authority, rotating credentials and transferring control.

## Established Observations

### Chapter 1

- Calvin requests observation access under a masked identity and enters under operational-minimum authority.
- `AUTHORITY: OBSERVATION` describes his ordinary local role; his central administrator status retains a narrow forensic override.
- His initial thought-code suspension of the local style layer is legitimate.
- The square restores itself after four seconds rather than the commanded thirty, and the audit attributes that unauthorised restoration to Calvin. This is the first permissions breach.
- The Sourceress studies the procedure and reproduces a related edit.
- The audit attributes her handprint edit to the world owner.
- An administrator pane appears without Calvin opening it, but the system reports only his granted access.

### Chapter 2

- Calvin genuinely returns to his central workspace.
- He seals BabyLondon, suspends outbound broadcast and blocks external processes while preserving resident continuity.
- The Sourceress remains located inside sealed BabyLondon with no cross-boundary process execution.
- The central permissions layer nevertheless recognises her as a secondary administrator with `AUTHORITY SOURCE: TRENT, CALVIN`.
- Suspension and complete credential rotation work temporarily.
- She reproduces the results of the new administrative procedures.
- The system later receives two valid identity claims from the same authority source.
- Emergency severance revokes Calvin rather than the Sourceress.
- She becomes the remaining system administrator and seals Calvin’s workspace.

## Governing Distinction

Her **presence** remains inside BabyLondon. Her **authority** exceeds the world’s boundary.

Do not replace this with a false-workspace reveal or physical teleportation.

## Learning Pattern

The Sourceress appears to learn the shape of authorised acts by watching Calvin exercise authority. The EverMind supplies or accepts whatever lies between visible form and valid execution.

This is an observable pattern, not a complete mechanism.

## Final Administrative State

After BabyLondon’s transformation, the Sourceress relinquishes embodied agency but her administrator privileges remain available to the Chorus. The privileges do not become CodeSong and do not make the Chorus morally good by technical inheritance.

## Narrative Function

Administrator recoding represents creation and control through command. Its failures expose the difference between technical legitimacy and moral wisdom.

## Limits

- Exact credential architecture remains unresolved.
- The reason the EverMind validates copied authority remains unresolved.
- The effect of permissions upon RealFonts should be established only as scenes require it.
- The governance of the final administrator privileges remains unresolved.

## Open Questions

- What normal safeguard is failing when authority crosses a sealed boundary?
- What can administrator rights do directly to a RealSoul’s simulated body or connection?
- Does the Sourceress copy verbs, identity, intention, permission topology or some deeper source-pattern?
- Can the Chorus revoke or constrain the privileges it receives?
- Does the Sourceress retain any capacity to withdraw them?

## Canonical Material

- Administrator access is not moral authority.
- Technical capability is not wisdom.
- Each containment procedure Calvin demonstrates becomes another possible lesson.
