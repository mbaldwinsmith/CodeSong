# Permissions and Administrator Rights

> **Status:** Placeholder  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## Definition

Administrator rights are permissions used by Calvin in his EverMind work. They permit direct recoding of simulated matter.

## Known Rules

- The Sourceress initially requires administrator rights to kill herself.
- Calvin possesses or uses administrator rights.
- The Sourceress learns from Calvin’s use of administrator permissions.
- Administrator rights permit the direct manipulation of simulated matter.
- The Sourceress’s copied capability gives her power to harm Calvin and control events in BabyLondon.

## Narrative Function

Administrator recoding represents creation and control through command, in contrast with CodeSong’s relational participation.

## Limits

The nature, safeguards, scope and revocability of administrator rights remain unresolved.

## Open Questions

- Are administrator rights credentials, knowledge, identity permissions or embodied capacities?
- Why can the Sourceress copy them?
- What prevents Calvin from revoking them?
- Can permissions affect RealFonts?

## Canonical Material

- Administrator access is not moral authority.
- Technical capability is not wisdom.
