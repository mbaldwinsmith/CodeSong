# CodeSong and the Chorus

> **Status:** Placeholder  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## Definition

CodeSong is a relational means of creating and transforming simulated reality through coordinated voices, attention and intention. The Chorus is the community of distinct voices that develops it.

## Known Rules

- CodeSong is distinct from administrator control and direct recoding.
- The Chorus creates through harmony while preserving distinct voices.
- Ethan and Everett guide the Chorus.
- The Chorus uses CodeSong to transform BabyLondon into a world of immense beauty and harmony.

## Narrative Function

CodeSong and the Chorus embody the movement from command and threatened assimilation towards participation and communion without loss of individuality.
