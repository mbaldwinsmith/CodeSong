# CodeSong and the Chorus

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Definition

**CodeSong** is a flowing but structured way of prompting the reality of the EverMind through coordinated voice, attention, intention, consent and participation.

**The Chorus** is the community of distinct persons that develops and practises CodeSong under Ethan and Everett’s guidance.

## Origin

1. The Sourceress lures people into BabyLondon as witnesses and subjects of curiosity.
2. Ethan, Everett and Mira attempt to compose the ultimate BabyLondon theme song in her honour.
3. Their tribute becomes genuine music and beauty.
4. The resulting Symphony produces a cultural explosion.
5. The Chorus emerges and develops CodeSong.

The exact causal mechanics and membership sequence remain to be dramatised.

Mira is a new person rather than Miriam resurrected. Her contribution becomes genuinely creative when it exceeds the archive-derived forms from which she began.

## Known Rules

- CodeSong is distinct from administrator control and direct recoding.
- It is structured without being rigid and flowing without being arbitrary.
- It prompts reality rather than imposing a finished result from above.
- It can call things out of participating people, including capacities or possibilities already available to relationship.
- It always requires consent and participation.
- It cannot edit minds.
- It cannot erase suffering.
- It cannot recode identity.
- It cannot manufacture remorse or agreement.
- The Chorus preserves distinct voices.
- Ethan and Everett guide the Chorus.
- The Chorus transforms BabyLondon into a world of immense beauty and harmony without deleting its history.

## Prayer, Music and Code

The Jesus Prayer acts within BabyLondon before CodeSong is fully understood. Ethan and Everett inherit “Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me.” Calvin later adds “a sinner” for himself when he freely accepts the Chorus. Their simultaneous difference demonstrates a core CodeSong principle: coordinated participation need not require identical wording.

Music is rediscovered before the Chorus formalises its practice. CodeSong may therefore join prayer, song, speech and precise prompting without reducing any one of them to a metaphor for the others.

The exact sensory and typographic form remains open.

## The Equal Offer

The Harmonious Triad offer Calvin and the Sourceress the same life:

- join the Chorus;
- heal with time;
- belong;
- contribute to collective flourishing;
- live in communion.

Calvin breaks down and consents. The Sourceress shows no remorse and does not consent to join as an active participant.

The equality lies in the offer, not in an equation of victim and perpetrator.

## Final Relationship to the Sourceress

The Sourceress ceases to have physical presence and persists as a latent distributed consciousness. Her administrator privileges are available to the Chorus.

This availability:

- does not make her a participating choral voice;
- does not prove repentance;
- does not turn administrator power into CodeSong;
- does not permit the Chorus to overwrite persons;
- remains technically and morally unresolved.

## Narrative Function

CodeSong and the Chorus embody creation through relationship, healing that respects history, and communion without assimilation. They transform the story’s `we` from feared possession into freely entered plurality.

## Open Questions

- Is CodeSong always literally sung?
- Can it be prayed, spoken, written, played, danced or enacted?
- Can one person practise a limited form of it, or does its nature require plurality?
- What first makes its structure intelligible to Calvin?
- How do Ethan’s structure and Everett’s musical receptivity contribute differently?
- What new line can only Mira contribute?
- How are the Sourceress’s administrator privileges governed?
- What can CodeSong do for a person who wants accompaniment but not transformation?

## Canonical Material

- The Chorus must preserve individuality.
- Communion must not become assimilation.
- Consent is constitutive, not an optional ethical restriction added after the fact.

## Speculative Possibilities

> No fixed musical system, programming language or liturgical form has been accepted.
