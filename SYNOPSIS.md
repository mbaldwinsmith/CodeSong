# Synopsis

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## One-Sentence Synopsis

An EverMind investigator enters an anomalous golden SimWorld, loses authority over his body and is forced to harm his family, while his sons and a growing Chorus discover a consensual mode of creation capable of transforming the world without rewriting the people wounded within it.

## Provisional Story Spine

1. Calvin Trent receives a scream formatted as an advertisement from BabyLondon.
2. He enters the sealed SimWorld and meets its only registered resident, a childlike SimSoul with an unstable celebrity-derived appearance.
3. She studies Calvin’s spoken and thought-code actions, then imitates the visible forms of his authority.
4. Calvin genuinely returns to his central workspace and seals BabyLondon.
5. When he names the case subject `SORCERESS`, she inserts the `U`, adopts `SOURCERESS`, and proves that her authority has spread beyond her world.
6. Calvin’s attempts at containment teach her the remaining procedures she needs to revoke his access, seal his workspace and capture his body. His first-person narration begins to fracture.
7. BabyLondon’s later population grows through two accepted movements whose relative timing remains open: the Sourceress lures people to witness her tributes and satisfy her curiosity, while Ethan, Everett and Rahma enter looking for Calvin. The exact means by which RealSouls enter remains deliberately unresolved.
8. The boys bring Rahma to comfort Calvin, provoking his fear and anger, which the Sourceress twists.
9. Calvin is tortured and dehumanised, then coerced into harming Rahma and his sons while continuing to love and comfort them.
10. Ethan and Everett, already practising the Eastern Catholic faith inherited from Miriam, pray the Jesus Prayer. Suffering and their tragic loyalty to Calvin awaken their faith into active resistance and witness.
11. Three BabyLondon inhabitants attempt to create the ultimate tribute song for the Sourceress and rediscover genuine music and beauty. They become the Harmonious Triad.
12. Their Symphony produces a cultural explosion and the emergence of the Chorus, in which distinct persons create together without assimilation.
13. Under Ethan and Everett’s guidance, the Chorus develops CodeSong: a flowing but structured, consensual way of prompting the EverMind that can call forth but never overwrite a person.
14. CodeSong transforms BabyLondon without erasing its history or repairing people by recoding their identities.
15. The Harmonious Triad offer Calvin and the Sourceress the same life within the Chorus: time to heal, belonging, contribution and communion.
16. Calvin breaks down and accepts. His recovery begins through participation rather than reset.
17. The Sourceress shows no remorse and does not accept the offer. She judges BabyLondon complete and no longer requires an embodied presence within it.
18. She severs her agency from her SimFont, becoming a latent distributed consciousness without physical presence. Her administrator privileges remain available to the Chorus.
19. The surviving community must learn how to flourish with unhealed history, Calvin’s continuing moral injury and the Sourceress’s unresolved distributed presence.

## Status Note

Chapters 1 and 2 are accepted working canon and remain open to revision. Events after Chapter 2 are accepted directional canon unless explicitly labelled unresolved; their chapter allocation, pacing and exact enactment remain provisional.
