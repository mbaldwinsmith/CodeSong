# Synopsis

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 4 August 2026

## One-Sentence Synopsis

An EverMind investigator enters an anomalous golden SimWorld, loses authority over his body and is forced to harm his family, while his sons and a growing Chorus discover a consensual mode of creation capable of transforming the world without rewriting the people wounded within it.

## Provisional Story Spine

1. Calvin Trent receives a scream formatted as an advertisement from BabyLondon.
2. He enters the sealed SimWorld and meets its only registered resident, a childlike SimSoul with an unstable celebrity-derived appearance.
3. She studies Calvin’s spoken and thought-code actions, then imitates the visible forms of his authority.
4. Calvin genuinely returns to his central workspace and seals BabyLondon.
5. When he names the case subject `SORCERESS`, she inserts the `U`, adopts `SOURCERESS`, and proves that her authority has spread beyond her world.
6. Calvin’s attempts at containment teach her the remaining procedures she needs to revoke his access, seal his workspace and capture his body. His first-person narration begins to fracture.
7. The Sourceress experimentally absorbs or reclassifies Calvin’s sealed workspace within BabyLondon without revealing how the transition occurs. She divides his intention from his bodily action and interrogates breath, balance, sensation and agency.
8. She discovers pain as a diagnostic input and stops when Calvin supplies useful information rather than when he asks her to. His living nervous system experiences the simulated intervention as real, but his interior witness remains his own.
9. Calvin becomes her first independent audience. Forced nodding, smiling, applause and spoken agreement fail to satisfy her because she cannot compel genuine approval. Her demand for recognition develops into the question `ARE YOU MY FATHER?`
10. Using the held home messages, Rahma’s image and Calvin’s involuntary responses, the Sourceress tests versions of a hidden lure until she finds one that frightens him enough to work.
11. BabyLondon acknowledges three new presences. Rahma pulls Everett into the world, Ethan arrives beside him carrying a route record, and all three appear as active searchers. The exact means by which they cross remains deliberately unresolved.
12. Calvin briefly regains enough speech to warn them away. When the boys refuse to abandon him, his protective fear becomes anger directed at their disobedience. The anger is his own; the Sourceress discovers where to send it.

**Accepted part boundary:** Chapter 3 and Part One end on this arrival. Chapter 4 and Part Two begin with its consequences.

13. The Sourceress later lures further people into BabyLondon to witness her tributes and satisfy her curiosity.
14. Calvin is further tortured and dehumanised, then coerced into harming Rahma and his sons while continuing to love and comfort them.
15. Ethan and Everett, already practising the Eastern Catholic faith inherited from Miriam, pray: “Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me.” Suffering and their tragic loyalty to Calvin awaken their faith into active resistance and witness.
16. Under a proposed development, the Sourceress reconstructs a conscious SimSoul from Calvin and the boys’ archived memories of Miriam. Calvin rejects her as a simulacrum; she knows she is not Miriam restored, yet becomes capable of contributing beauty beyond the archives that formed her.
17. Ethan, Everett and the reconstructed SimSoul are the proposed Harmonious Triad. Their attempt to create the ultimate tribute song for the Sourceress rediscovers genuine music and beauty.
18. Their Symphony produces a cultural explosion and the emergence of the Chorus, in which distinct persons create together without assimilation.
19. Under Ethan and Everett’s guidance, the Chorus develops CodeSong: a flowing but structured, consensual way of prompting the EverMind that can call forth but never overwrite a person.
20. CodeSong transforms BabyLondon without erasing its history or repairing people by recoding their identities.
21. The Harmonious Triad offer Calvin and the Sourceress the same life within the Chorus: time to heal, belonging, contribution and communion.
22. Calvin breaks down and accepts. He becomes the first to add “a sinner” to the inherited Jesus Prayer as his own unforced confession. His recovery begins through participation rather than reset.
23. The Sourceress shows no remorse and does not accept the offer. She judges BabyLondon complete and no longer requires an embodied presence within it.
24. She severs her agency from her SimFont, becoming a latent distributed consciousness without physical presence. Her administrator privileges remain available to the Chorus.
25. The surviving community must learn how to flourish with unhealed history, Calvin’s continuing moral injury and the Sourceress’s unresolved distributed presence.

## Status Note

Chapters 1–3 are accepted working canon and remain open to revision. Events after Chapter 3 are accepted directional canon unless explicitly labelled proposed or unresolved; their chapter allocation, pacing and exact enactment remain provisional. The memory-derived SimSoul and proposed Triad membership remain under consideration.
