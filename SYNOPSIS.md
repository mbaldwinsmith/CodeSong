# Synopsis

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## One-Sentence Synopsis

An EverMind investigator enters an anomalous golden SimWorld, is stripped of control over his own body and forced to harm his family, while his sons and a newly formed Chorus discover a mode of creation powerful enough to transform the world without reproducing its violence.

## Provisional Story Spine

1. Calvin Trent receives an anomalous broadcast from BabyLondon.
2. He enters the SimWorld to investigate.
3. He encounters the Sourceress and initially underestimates her.
4. She studies his administrator actions and learns to alter simulated matter.
5. She traps and compromises him.
6. Calvin’s narrative voice begins to dissociate from first person into third person.
7. Ethan, Everett and Rahma are brought into BabyLondon.
8. The Sourceress coerces Calvin into harming those he loves.
9. Ethan and Everett preserve their love for Calvin and begin praying within the simulation.
10. Three inhabitants attempt to create a theme song for BabyLondon.
11. They rediscover music and become the Harmonious Triad.
12. Their work leads to the Symphony and the formation of the Chorus.
13. Ethan and Everett guide the Chorus in developing CodeSong.
14. CodeSong transforms BabyLondon through relationship rather than command.
15. Calvin begins to recover the capacity to speak as “I.”
16. The Sourceress recognises BabyLondon as a fitting tribute to her idols.
17. She destroys her own SimFont despite being offered another possible way to belong.
18. The surviving characters must decide how to live within what remains.

## Warning

This synopsis is provisional. It records the current narrative movement but must not be treated as fixed canon.
