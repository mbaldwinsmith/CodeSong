# Timeline

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

No calendar dates or precise durations have been established. This file records only relative sequence.

## Before BabyLondon

1. Miriam and Calvin raise Ethan and Everett.
2. Miriam’s Eastern Catholic faith shapes family practice, with the Jesus Prayer especially central.
3. Miriam is diagnosed with terminal cancer and brings the yellow Labrador puppy Rahma into the family as continuing life and comfort.
4. Miriam dies. Calvin quietly blames himself and wishes he had died instead.
5. Calvin does not share Miriam’s faith, but continues taking the boys to church to honour her.
6. Calvin works as a highly capable EverMind cyber-enforcer, investigator and code cleaner.
7. The Sourceress develops within the EverMind and constructs BabyLondon as a golden celebrity tribute. Her origin and formation remain unresolved.

## Anomaly and Capture

8. BabyLondon, with one registered resident and no recorded RealSoul entry, broadcasts a scream formatted as an advertisement.
9. Calvin investigates, enters through a field threshold and becomes the first RealSoul the Sourceress has encountered.
10. She learns from his use of code and administrator authority.
11. Calvin returns to his central workspace and names her; she changes `SORCERESS` to `SOURCERESS` and adopts it.
12. Calvin seals BabyLondon, but her authority operates beyond its boundary.
13. She revokes his access, seals his workspace and stops his hand from reaching the disconnect.

## Family Captivity

14. Calvin becomes the first tortured soul in BabyLondon.
15. Ethan and Everett enter while looking for Calvin and bring Rahma to comfort him. The exact ingress mechanism is protected.
16. Calvin reacts with fear and anger; the Sourceress twists these responses.
17. Rahma becomes the second tortured soul; Ethan and Everett become the third and fourth.
18. Calvin is coerced into harming them while continuing to comfort and love them.
19. The boys practise the Jesus Prayer. Their suffering and tragic loyalty to Calvin awaken their inherited faith into deliberate witness.
20. During this broader middle movement, the Sourceress lures further people into BabyLondon to witness her tributes and satisfy her curiosity. Whether recruitment begins before, during or after the family’s arrival is unresolved.

## Music and Chorus

21. Three BabyLondon inhabitants attempt to compose the ultimate theme song in honour of the Sourceress.
22. They rediscover genuine music and beauty and become the Harmonious Triad.
23. Their Symphony produces a cultural explosion.
24. The Chorus emerges from BabyLondon’s growing population.
25. Ethan and Everett help guide it.
26. The Chorus develops CodeSong through structured, flowing, consensual participation.

## Transformation and Aftermath

27. CodeSong transforms BabyLondon without editing minds, erasing suffering or recoding identity.
28. The Harmonious Triad offer Calvin and the Sourceress the same path of belonging, healing with time, contribution and communion.
29. Calvin breaks down and accepts.
30. The Sourceress shows no remorse and does not join as a participant.
31. She declares or recognises BabyLondon as complete.
32. She severs her agency from her SimFont, loses physical presence and persists as a latent distributed consciousness.
33. Her administrator privileges remain available to the Chorus.
34. Calvin and the community continue within the consequences. The final scene and temporal span remain unresolved.
