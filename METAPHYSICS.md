# Metaphysics

> **Status:** In development  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Working Proposition

> Any being capable of suffering, loving, remembering, choosing and entering reciprocal relationship must be treated as a person within the moral world of the story.

This remains a working moral proposition rather than an in-world doctrinal statement.

## Personhood

Personhood is not restricted to biological substrate. RealSouls and SimSouls can both suffer, love, choose, remember and participate in relationship.

No person may be reduced to editable code merely because their sustaining pattern is computational.

## Consciousness

A SoulFont is the generative source-pattern of a conscious person, not a simple document containing a finished identity. The exact relationship between SoulFont, experience and consciousness remains unresolved.

The Sourceress’s final condition establishes that consciousness, embodied presence, agency and administrator authority may become distinguishable without being cleanly separable in explanatory terms.

## Embodiment

RealSouls remain rooted in biological bodies outside the EverMind while experiencing simulated embodiment within it. SimSouls possess computational SimFonts and can have bodies rendered within SimWorlds.

Embodied action matters morally even when bodily authorship is stolen. The story must hold together the reality of harm, Calvin’s coercion and the continuing relational consequences.

## Continuity of Identity

Identity cannot be recoded into moral wholeness. CodeSong cannot overwrite memory, personality, suffering or history. Healing occurs through time, consent, relationship and participation.

The identity implications of copying, backup, migration and distributed consciousness remain open.

## Suffering

Suffering is neither proof of holiness nor raw material to be erased after it has served the plot. Repeated resuscitation is not restoration, and transformed surroundings do not undo trauma.

## Moral Agency and Coercion

Agency admits degrees and can be violated. Calvin is not freely choosing every action enacted through him, yet technical innocence cannot by itself repair his moral injury or damaged relationships.

The Sourceress’s malformed formation explains aspects of her conduct without cancelling her responsibility. Her final absence of remorse is canonical.

## Death Within Simulation

Death in the EverMind can kill a RealSoul’s biological body. What constitutes death for a SimSoul remains unsettled.

The Sourceress does not die in the accepted ending. She severs her agency from her SimFont and ceases to have a physical presence, while persisting as a latent distributed consciousness.

## Command and Participation

Administrator authority changes simulated reality through authenticated command. CodeSong is a flowing but structured way of prompting the EverMind through coordinated attention, voice, consent and participation.

CodeSong can call things out of participating persons, including capacities or possibilities already available to relationship. It cannot:

- edit a mind;
- erase suffering;
- recode identity;
- compel participation;
- manufacture remorse;
- restore a person by reverting them to a prior state.

## Individuality and Communion

The Chorus is not a hive mind. Its harmony depends upon distinct voices remaining distinct. Communion increases intelligibility without making persons interchangeable.

Calvin accepts the Triad’s offer to belong, heal with time and contribute to collective flourishing. The Sourceress receives the same offer and does not accept it as a participant.

## Protected Mysteries

- The exact means by which RealSouls enter the EverMind.
- The ultimate relation between SoulFont and personhood.
- The precise mechanism by which the Sourceress copies Calvin’s authority.
- The deepest technical or metaphysical operation of CodeSong.
- The subjective experience of the Sourceress’s final distributed state.
