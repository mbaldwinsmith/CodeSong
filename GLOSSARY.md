# Glossary

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

Entries are provisional and alphabetised without regard to a leading “the”.

## Administrator Rights

Permissions used by Calvin in his work that permit direct recoding of simulated matter. Their precise mechanics and limits remain unresolved.

## BabyLondon

The Sourceress’s golden SimWorld, created as a tribute to celebrity consumer culture.

## Chorus, the

A community of distinct voices, guided by Ethan and Everett, that develops CodeSong and transforms BabyLondon through harmony.

## CodeSong

A relational means of creating and transforming simulated reality through coordinated voices, attention and intention.

## EverMind

An advanced simulation ecology containing billions of RealSouls, quadrillions of SimSouls and millions of SimWorlds.

## Harmonious Triad, the

Three figures within BabyLondon whose attempt to create its ultimate theme song leads to the rediscovery of genuine music and beauty.

## RealFont

A biological SoulFont rooted in a living nervous system outside the EverMind.

## RealSoul

A living person whose biological SoulFont remains rooted in the physical world while synchronised with the EverMind.

## SimFont

A computational SoulFont that can operate on a sufficiently compatible substrate.

## Simulated Matter

Matter within a SimWorld that can be recoded; its exact mechanics remain provisional.

## SimSoul

A person entirely contained within the EverMind whose SoulFont is computational rather than biological.

## SimWorld

A bounded experiential world operating within the EverMind.

## SoulFont

The generative source-pattern from which a conscious person arises.

## Sourceress, the

The childlike SimSoul who created BabyLondon, learned to recode its simulated matter and ultimately wipes her own SimFont.

## Symphony, the

The creative breakthrough produced by the Harmonious Triad while attempting to write BabyLondon’s ultimate theme song.
