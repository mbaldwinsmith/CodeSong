# Glossary

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

Entries are alphabetised without regard to a leading “the”. Definitions state current observable canon without pretending to settle protected mechanisms.

## Administrator Rights

Authenticated authority used by Calvin in his work to alter permissions and simulated matter. The Sourceress learns the shape of authorised acts by observing him and becomes recognised as an administrator beyond BabyLondon’s sealed boundary. Administrator access is not moral authority.

## BabyLondon

The Sourceress’s golden SimWorld, created as a tribute to celebrity consumer culture. It begins with one registered resident, later gains people lured as witnesses and subjects of curiosity, and is transformed by the Chorus without its history being erased.

## Central Workspace

Calvin’s genuine EverMind work environment outside BabyLondon. He returns to it through a functioning field threshold in Chapter 2, where the Sourceress’s presence remains contained but her authority does not.

## Chorus, the

A community of distinct consenting voices, guided by Ethan and Everett, that develops CodeSong. It creates in communion without assimilating its participants.

## CodeSong

A flowing but structured way of prompting the reality of the EverMind through coordinated voices, attention, intention, consent and participation. It can call possibilities out of people and worlds, but cannot edit minds, erase suffering or recode identity.

## EverMind

An advanced simulation ecology containing billions of RealSouls, quadrillions of SimSouls and millions of SimWorlds.

## Field Threshold

A controlled passage between an EverMind workspace and a SimWorld. Calvin’s threshold successfully returns him from BabyLondon; the later breach concerns jurisdiction and authority rather than the Sourceress physically crossing it.

## Harmonious Triad, the

Three figures within BabyLondon whose attempt to create its ultimate theme song leads to genuine music, the Symphony and the Chorus. They later make the same offer of communion to Calvin and the Sourceress.

## Jesus Prayer, the

The central prayer practice inherited by Ethan and Everett from Miriam’s Eastern Catholic faith. Its exact wording and on-page rhythm have not yet been fixed.

## RealFont

A biological SoulFont rooted in a living nervous system outside the EverMind.

## RealSoul

A living person whose biological SoulFont remains rooted in the physical world while synchronised with the EverMind.

## SimFont

A computational SoulFont capable of sustaining a SimSoul on a compatible substrate. In the accepted ending, the Sourceress does not erase hers; she separates her active agency from it and persists latently and distributively.

## SimSoul

A person entirely contained within the EverMind whose SoulFont is computational rather than biological.

## SimWorld

A bounded experiential world operating within the EverMind.

## SoulFont

The generative source-pattern from which a conscious person arises. It is not established as an editable file containing a complete identity.

## Sourceress, the

The childlike SimSoul who created BabyLondon. Calvin types `SORCERESS` as a provisional case designation; she inserts the `U`, adopts `SOURCERESS`, and turns a classification into her first stable received identity.

## Symphony, the

The creative breakthrough produced by the Harmonious Triad while attempting to write BabyLondon’s ultimate theme song. It leads to a cultural explosion and the formation of the Chorus.
