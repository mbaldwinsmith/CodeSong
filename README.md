# CodeSong

**CodeSong** is a techno-mythic fiction project developed through disciplined human–AI collaboration.

It concerns Calvin Trent, a cyber-enforcer working within the EverMind; the anomalous SimWorld BabyLondon; the childlike and dangerous Sourceress; Calvin’s family; and the emergence of CodeSong, a relational form of creation practised by the Chorus.

## Central Creative Principle

> The AI clarifies the pattern; the human author decides what it means.

The human author retains final creative, moral and symbolic authority.

## Current Phase

Foundation and discovery.

The project is currently preserving its original dream material, defining its creative vision, developing its narrative voice and establishing the initial story architecture.
