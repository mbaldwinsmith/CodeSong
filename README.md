# CodeSong

**CodeSong** is a techno-mythic fiction project developed through disciplined human–AI collaboration.

It concerns Calvin Trent, an EverMind cyber-enforcer; the anomalous golden SimWorld BabyLondon; the childlike and dangerous Sourceress; Calvin’s family; and the emergence of a relational form of creation practised by the Chorus.

## Central Creative Principle

> The AI clarifies the pattern; the human author decides what it means.

The human author retains final creative, moral and symbolic authority.

## Current Phase

Accepted opening movement and documentation.

Chapters 1–3 are accepted working canon while remaining open to revision. The next creative task is to define the scene boundary after the family’s arrival.
