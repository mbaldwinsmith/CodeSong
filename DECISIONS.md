## Recorded Decisions

### D-001: Human creative authority

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
The human author retains final authority over creative, symbolic, moral and theological decisions.

**Reason:**  
AI participation is intended to clarify and strengthen the work, not replace human authorship.

**Consequences:**  
AI-generated material remains provisional until accepted.

**Related files:**  
All files.

### D-002: Evolving perspective

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
The narrative will evolve from Calvin’s first-person voice, through fractured and dissociated forms, towards polyphonic choral narration.

**Reason:**  
The form should enact the theft and recovery of personhood.

**Consequences:**  
Perspective shifts must be planned and tracked across the story.

**Related files:**  
`STYLE.md`, `NARRATIVE-SYSTEM.md`, `STRUCTURE.md`.

### D-003: Calvin as initial narrator

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
The story begins from Calvin Trent’s first-person perspective.

**Reason:**  
Calvin’s initial narrative ownership makes his later loss of agency more immediate and formally powerful.

**Consequences:**  
The opening must establish a distinctive, stable first-person voice.

**Related files:**  
`chapters/01-anomaly.md`, `STYLE.md`.

### D-004: Story before system

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
Documentation should arise from and support living story material rather than replacing creative discovery.

**Reason:**  
Over-formalisation may flatten dream logic, ambiguity and emotional vitality.

**Consequences:**  
The opening calibration scene should be drafted before the full documentation system is treated as complete.

**Related files:**  
`TASKS.md`, `chapters/01-anomaly.md`.
