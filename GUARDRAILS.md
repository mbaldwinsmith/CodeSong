# Guardrails

> **Status:** Provisional  
> **Authority:** Human author  
> **Last reviewed:** 3 August 2026

## Moral and Narrative Guardrails

1. The Sourceress’s original plan of self-sacrifice is tragic and malformed, not inherently redemptive.
2. The accepted ending is not her death, repentance or purification. She remains conscious in a latent distributed form after relinquishing embodied agency.
3. Her decision to make administrator privileges available to the Chorus must not retroactively redeem her abuses.
4. The Harmonious Triad make a genuine equivalent offer to Calvin and the Sourceress. Neither response is compelled.
5. The Sourceress does not show remorse. Do not supply remorse merely to make the ending emotionally tidy.
6. Calvin’s coercion matters morally.
7. Calvin must not be treated as though he freely chose every action performed through his body.
8. Calvin’s moral injury must not be erased merely by establishing his technical innocence.
9. Calvin’s acceptance of the Chorus begins healing over time; it does not reset him.
10. Forgiveness must not require denial of harm.
11. Ethan and Everett are not admirable because they suffer without protest.
12. The boys must remain recognisable children with fear, anger, humour, needs and distinct personalities.
13. Their awakened faith must not imply that their suffering was necessary, deserved or good.
14. Rahma’s suffering must not be used repeatedly for emotional escalation.
15. Repeated resuscitation is not restoration.
16. SimSouls are persons, not disposable metaphors for the development of RealSouls.
17. The Chorus must preserve individuality.
18. Communion must not become assimilation.
19. CodeSong always requires consent and participation.
20. CodeSong cannot edit minds, erase suffering, recode identity or manufacture virtue.
21. Beauty is not automatic evidence of goodness.
22. Administrator access is not moral authority.
23. Technical capability is not wisdom.
24. No person is merely corrupted code awaiting deletion.
25. The Sourceress must remain morally accountable without being reduced to a simple monster.
26. Her formation by celebrity consumer culture explains aspects of her condition but does not erase her agency.
27. Christian elements should emerge through prayer, action, relationship and material consequence rather than argument.
28. Hope must not require the story to pretend that irreversible damage never occurred.

## Mystery Guardrails

- Do not explain exactly how RealSouls enter the EverMind merely to complete the worldbuilding.
- Do not reduce the Sourceress’s copied authority to a conventional credential exploit unless the author decides to do so.
- Do not define CodeSong so mechanically that consent, relation and discovery become cosmetic.
- Describe the observable conditions of the Sourceress’s final state without claiming access to an explanation the story has not earned.

## Process Guardrails

- Do not silently alter accepted canon.
- Do not overwrite human-authored prose without direct instruction.
- Present major rewrites as proposals or patches.
- Record settled decisions.
- Separate canonical material from speculation.
- Mark AI-generated additions clearly until accepted.
- Do not reopen locked decisions without new evidence or human instruction.
